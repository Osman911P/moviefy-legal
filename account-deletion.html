Delete your Moviefy account

Last updated: October 2026

Moviefy lets you permanently delete your account and associated personal data directly in the app.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Moviefy and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom navigation).
  3. Tap Settings (edit profile / settings screen).
  4. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the prompts, type DELETE when asked, and complete deletion.

Deletion removes your auth account and associated Moviefy data (profile, library, reviews, follows, blocks, reports you submitted, and uploaded profile images) according to our backend configuration.

Cannot access the app?

Email us from the address linked to your account:

moviefysupport@gmail.com

Subject: Moviefy Account Deletion Request

Include your Moviefy username and the email address on the account. We will verify ownership and process deletion within a reasonable time.
