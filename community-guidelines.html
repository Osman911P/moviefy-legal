Moviefy Community Guidelines

Moviefy is built for people who love movies and TV. Public reviews and profiles must stay respectful and lawful. By using social features, you agree to follow these rules.

Do not post or encourage

  • Harassment, bullying, or targeted abuse
  • Threats or encouragement of violence
  • Hate speech or slurs against protected groups
  • Sexually explicit content
  • Illegal activity or instructions to commit crimes
  • Spam, scams, or deceptive links
  • Impersonation of other people or brands
  • Sharing someone else’s private personal information
  • Deliberately offensive or abusive material intended to harm others

Reviews and ratings

Focus on the title you are reviewing. Spoilers are allowed but be considerate. Do not use reviews primarily to attack other users.

Reporting and blocking

Use in-app Report for policy violations. Use Block to hide someone from your experience. Misusing reports may lead to action on your account.

Enforcement

We may remove content or restrict accounts that break these guidelines or our Terms of Use. Serious or repeated violations can result in permanent removal.
