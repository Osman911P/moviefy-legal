Moviefy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy describes how Moviefy (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Moviefy mobile application and related services. Moviefy is a movie and TV tracking and social app.

Contact

Developer / Operator: Osman Peçenek
App: Moviefy
Privacy contact: moviefysupport@gmail.com

Privacy questions and requests: use the email above.

Information you provide

  • Account: email address, password (stored by our auth provider), optional Google Sign-In identifiers.
  • Profile: username, bio, profile and banner images you upload.
  • Activity: movies/series in your library (watched, watchlist, favorites), episode watch progress, ratings and text reviews you publish.
  • Social: follow relationships, blocks, and reports you submit about users or reviews.
  • Preferences: app language (English/Turkish).

Information from third parties

  • TMDB: movie and TV metadata, posters, and related information displayed in the app. We send search and detail requests to TMDB using our API credentials. TMDB’s policies apply to their services.
  • Google: if you use Google Sign-In, Google processes authentication according to Google’s policies.

How we use information

We use data to operate Moviefy: authenticate you, store your library and social graph, display public reviews, enforce community rules, improve reliability, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the service, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, and server functions.
  • Google — optional sign-in.
  • TMDB — entertainment metadata (not a host for your account data).

These providers process data on our behalf under their terms and security practices.

Public content

Reviews you publish, your username, avatar, and public profile statistics may be visible to other Moviefy users. Do not post personal information you do not want to be public.

Storage and security

Profile images are stored in secured cloud storage with access controls. We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS). No method of storage is 100% secure; we work to protect your data proportionate to the risk.

Retention

We retain information while your account is active. Moderation reports may be retained for a reasonable period to investigate abuse. When you delete your account, associated personal data is removed according to our deletion process (see Account Deletion page).

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account. See our Account Deletion page for details and email fallback.

Your choices

You can update your profile, block users, and delete your account in the app. Contact us at the email above for other privacy requests where applicable law provides rights.

Children

Moviefy is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with an updated date. Material changes may require renewed acceptance in the app.
